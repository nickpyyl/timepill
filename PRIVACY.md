# timepill Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

timepill is a browser extension that converts selected time text and timezones inline in the browser.

## Data collection

timepill does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit personal data.

Selected text is processed locally in your browser to detect times, timezones, and city names. The selected text is not sent to a server.

## Stored settings

timepill stores only extension preferences using browser extension storage:

- Target timezone
- 12-hour or 24-hour display preference

These settings stay in your browser profile or browser sync storage, depending on your browser configuration.

## Network requests

timepill does not make network requests for analytics, tracking, advertising, or conversion.

The settings panel includes an optional external link to the author's X profile. Opening that link is a normal browser navigation controlled by you.

## Third parties

timepill does not share data with third parties.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the maintainer through the project repository or the X profile linked in the extension.
